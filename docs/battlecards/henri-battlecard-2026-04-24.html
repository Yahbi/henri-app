Angi

Founded
1995 (Angie's List)
Parent
Angi Inc. (NASDAQ: ANGI)
Headcount
~2,450 (post Q1 2026 cuts)
Target
U.S. home-services pros + homeowners
Position
Legacy incumbent, declining revenue

Pricing Pay-per-lead + annual membership

~$288–$300/yr access fee plus $25–$120 per lead by trade and metro. HVAC ~$65–$85; roofing $100+. Typical contractor spend $300–$2,500/mo. Contracts often 12-month auto-renew with 30–35% early-cancel penalty. Sources: LeadTruffle 2026, Hook Agency, Pipeline On.

What they sell

Two-sided marketplace: homeowners post jobs; Angi sells those leads to contractors, typically matching one homeowner to "up to four local pros" simultaneously. Adjacent products: reviews, profile listings, payments / invoicing, Angi Services (fixed-price jobs).

Their positioning

"Get jobs done well by connecting homeowners with reliable pros and connecting pros with homeowners who have the jobs they want." (Angi Careers)

Recent releases (last 90 days)

  • Mar 4, 2026 — Launched the Angi App inside ChatGPT for AI-guided homeowner-to-pro matching. (Angi IR)
  • Feb 10, 2026 — Q4 2025 earnings + shareholder letter from CEO Jeffrey Kip. (Angi IR)
  • Jan 7, 2026 — SEC filing: cutting ~350 jobs (12.5% of staff, mostly engineering), citing "AI-driven efficiency"; expects $70–80M annualized savings, $22–30M restructuring charge. (IBJ)

Where they win

Brand recognition + homeowner traffic. Decades of TV/SEO spend. Counter: Henri doesn't compete for homeowner mindshare; we surface contractors before the homeowner form-fills, by triggering on the permit.
Breadth of trades + national footprint. Counter: Henri is permit-driven across 46 states; coverage is the live record, not a directory.
Adjacent tools (payments, reviews, invoicing). Counter: Henri integrates with the contractor's existing stack; we focus on the lead, not the back-office.

Where Henri wins

Exclusivity. One contractor per ZIP per trade for 14 days, with 72-hour use-it-or-lose-it auto-release. Angi sells the same lead to "up to four local pros" by design.
Permits-first sourcing. Henri catches the homeowner at the permit pull, before they form-fill on a marketplace.
Transparent 6-signal scoring. Every lead drawer shows freshness, value, contact-completeness, ZIP demand, engagement, historical conversion with weights. Angi's match algorithm is opaque.
Flat monthly + cancel anytime. $149–$2,555 flat; no auto-renewing 12-month contract, no 30–35% early-cancel penalty, no per-lead surprise charges. The FTC's $7.2M settlement against HomeAdvisor (FTC v. HomeAdvisor, file no. 192-3124, 2023) was specifically about misrepresented lead quality — Henri shows the source permit so the contractor verifies it themselves.

Talk tracks

Early mention
Angi sells the same homeowner to up to four pros at once. Henri locks the permit to one contractor per ZIP per trade for 14 days. Different product, same trade — exclusivity vs. auction.
Displacement
Keep Angi if it's working — but run Henri's permit feed alongside for 30 days. You'll see leads Angi never gets, because we trigger off the permit, not the form-fill. Cancel anytime, no contract.
Late addition
If you've evaluated Angi, Thumbtack, and HomeAdvisor, you've seen the shared-lead model. Henri is the only feed where the lead is yours alone for 14 days, and you can see exactly why it scored 78.

Objections + responses

Angi has more brand recognition with homeowners.
True, and homeowners will keep finding Angi. Henri isn't a homeowner brand; we're the contractor's permit intelligence. Different category.
Angi is cheaper per lead.
Angi's $25–$120 per lead is paid 3–8 times by 3–8 contractors competing for the same homeowner. Henri's $149–$2,555 flat = unlimited permits in your ZIPs, exclusive for 14 days. Math flips at ~6 leads/month.
I've already paid Angi for the year.
Run them in parallel. Henri is month-to-month, no lock-in. If we don't earn the seat in 30 days, cancel. Angi's contract isn't a reason to skip the comparison.
How do I know your data is real?
Every lead links back to its source permit number, applicant name, scope, and value. We don't synthesize leads — we surface public records and enrich them. The score breakdown is visible in every drawer.

Landmine questions

  2. Walk me through the last time Angi gave you a refund credit for a bad lead — how long did that take, and how often did you actually get one?
  3. If you had to pause Angi tomorrow because work slowed down, what would canceling cost you?
